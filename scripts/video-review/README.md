# Hero video cut — 2026-09-09

Source: real AFS shop-floor footage of the Thalmann folding machine, shot 2026-09-08 (five clips) and 2026-08-14 (three clips). Raw clips are not committed (see .gitignore).

## hero-metal-fabrication.mp4 (18.2 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, silent, H.264)
1. FEED    — clip 1 (rear angle, 30 fps), 2.0–8.0 s: operator feeding the sheet into the machine.
2. BEND    — clip 2 (front-left angle, 120 fps), 13.0–17.0 s, slowed 1.75x: folding beam raises and forms the bend.
3. RELEASE — clip 4 (front-right angle, 30 fps), 0.5–6.5 s: beam lowers and the formed piece is released.
Segments joined with 0.4 s crossfades. Poster: public/images/hero-poster.jpg (frame at 7 s, mid-bend).

## shop-floor-loop.mp4 (25.0 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, silent, H.264)
Clip 1 (rear wide angle), 1.0–25.0 s, with the final second crossfaded into the clip's opening frames so the loop point is soft. Poster: public/images/shop-floor-poster.jpg.

Vertical clips (3, 5, 6, 7, 8) were not used: cropping 9:16 phone footage to 16:9 would have required upscaling.
